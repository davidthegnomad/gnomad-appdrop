Gnomad AppDrop — START HERE
====================

1. Double-click:  Install Gnomad AppDrop
   (On GNOME: if it asks, choose "Allow Launching" or right-click → Run as Program)

2. Read the splash screen → click Install.

3. When it finishes, click Open Gnomad AppDrop.

After that:
• Drop .AppImage or .tar.gz files into your Applications folder
• They show up in your app menu automatically
